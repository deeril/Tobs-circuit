### 1. INTRODUCTION AND ACCEPTANCE
These Driver and Rider Terms and Conditions ("Agreement") govern the relationship between DropLag ("the Company", "we", "us" or "our") and every driver, rider, delivery partner or independent delivery service provider ("the Driver").

By registering on the DropLag platform, accepting a delivery assignment, accessing the Driver Dashboard or using any Company service, the Driver confirms that they have read, understood and agreed to comply with this Agreement.

If the Driver does not agree to these terms, they must not register, access the platform or accept delivery assignments.

### 2. ELIGIBILITY AND REGISTRATION REQUIREMENTS
Every Driver must:

1. Be at least 18 years of age.
2. Provide accurate personal information during registration.
3. Provide a valid means of identification.
4. Provide a valid driver's licence appropriate for the vehicle being operated.
5. Provide valid vehicle registration and roadworthiness documentation, where applicable.
6. Provide valid vehicle insurance as required by Nigerian law.
7. Provide a current residential address, telephone number and emergency contact.
8. Submit a recent passport photograph.
9. Provide any additional documents reasonably required for verification.
10. Complete any required onboarding, safety training or operational assessment.
The Company reserves the right to verify submitted information and reject or suspend any application containing false, misleading, incomplete or fraudulent information.

### 3. DRIVER RESPONSIBILITIES
Every Driver agrees to:

- Accept and complete assigned deliveries professionally.
- Follow the delivery instructions provided through the platform.
- Collect parcels only from authorised pickup locations.
- Deliver parcels exclusively to the designated recipient or authorised representative.
- Maintain communication with the dispatch or operations team.
- Provide accurate pickup and delivery confirmation.
- Obtain the required recipient's confirmation or proof of delivery.
- Protect all packages from theft, damage, contamination or unauthorised access.
- Keep the vehicle clean, roadworthy and suitable for deliveries.
- Treat customers, merchants, staff and other road users respectfully.
- Report delays, accidents, breakdowns, theft or other incidents immediately.
- Comply with applicable traffic laws and road safety regulations.
Drivers must not subcontract, transfer or assign their delivery responsibilities to another person without written approval from the Company.

### 4. PROHIBITED CONDUCT
The following activities are strictly prohibited:

1. Stealing, opening, tampering with or unlawfully retaining customer parcels.
2. Collecting unauthorised payments from customers.
3. Demanding additional delivery fees outside the approved pricing.
4. Falsifying delivery confirmation, signatures, photographs or GPS information.
5. Sharing Driver Dashboard login credentials with another person.
6. Using another person's identity or driver's licence.
7. Harassing, threatening, intimidating or abusing customers or staff.
8. Carrying prohibited, illegal or undeclared goods.
9. Using the platform to facilitate criminal activity.
10. Accepting an assignment and deliberately failing to complete it.
11. Using customer information for personal marketing or unauthorised purposes.
12. Operating under the influence of alcohol, narcotics or any substance that impairs driving.
13. Engaging in reckless driving or any activity that endangers the public.
14. Manipulating delivery records, earnings, location information or payment records.
Any of these activities may result in immediate suspension, termination of platform access and referral to the appropriate authorities where legally justified.

### 5. DELIVERY ASSIGNMENTS AND SERVICE STANDARDS
Once a Driver accepts an assignment, the Driver is responsible for making reasonable efforts to complete it within the stated delivery timeframe.

The Driver must:

- Verify the parcel and pickup details before departure.
- Confirm collection through the platform.
- Follow the approved delivery route, subject to reasonable safety and road conditions.
- Avoid unnecessary diversions.
- Notify the Company of any expected delay.
- Confirm successful delivery using the approved proof-of-delivery process.
A Driver must not mark an order as delivered before the recipient has received it.

Where delivery cannot be completed, the Driver must contact the operations team and await further instructions.

### 6. CUSTOMER PROPERTY AND PARCEL RESPONSIBILITY
The Driver must exercise reasonable care over every parcel entrusted to them.

The Driver must not:

- Open or inspect parcel contents without lawful authority or Company approval.
- Leave parcels unattended in unauthorised locations.
- Transfer parcels to unauthorised individuals.
- Expose parcels to avoidable damage, rain, heat or contamination.
- Retain parcels after an unsuccessful delivery without informing the Company.
Where a parcel is lost, stolen or damaged while under the Driver's custody, the Driver must report the incident immediately and cooperate fully with the investigation.

Liability will be determined based on the available evidence, the Driver's conduct, the applicable law and any relevant insurance arrangements. The Company will not impose automatic penalties without a reasonable investigation.

### 7. PAYMENTS, EARNINGS AND REMITTANCES
The Driver's earnings, commissions, delivery fees and applicable deductions will be communicated through the approved payment arrangement.

The Driver agrees that:

1. All customer payments collected on behalf of the Company must be accurately recorded.
2. Cash collected must be remitted within the timeframe communicated by the Company.
3. Drivers must not retain, divert or personally use Company or customer funds.
4. All payment discrepancies must be reported immediately.
5. The Company may investigate suspicious transactions and reconcile delivery records.
6. Any lawful deductions must be properly explained and supported by relevant records.
The Company shall not make arbitrary deductions from earnings.

Any agreed service charges, commissions or other financial arrangements must be communicated before the Driver accepts the applicable engagement.

### 8. ACCIDENTS, INSURANCE AND ROAD SAFETY
The Driver is responsible for operating their vehicle safely and in compliance with applicable Nigerian traffic laws.

The Driver must:

- Maintain a valid driver's licence.
- Maintain legally required vehicle insurance.
- Ensure that the vehicle is roadworthy.
- Wear appropriate protective equipment, including an approved helmet when operating a motorcycle.
- Observe speed limits and traffic signs.
- Avoid distracted driving and mobile phone use while driving.
- Never operate a vehicle while impaired.
In the event of an accident, the Driver must:

1. Stop and take reasonable steps to protect life and prevent further injury.
2. Contact emergency services where necessary.
3. Notify DropLag immediately.
4. Report the incident to the relevant authorities where required.
5. Provide accurate information and cooperate with any investigation.
6. Submit relevant photographs, reports and insurance information where available.
The Driver must not admit legal liability on behalf of the Company or make unauthorised promises of compensation.

### 9. CUSTOMER INFORMATION AND DATA PROTECTION
All customer information accessed through DropLag is confidential.

The Driver must:

- Use customer names, phone numbers and addresses only to complete assigned deliveries.
- Not copy, sell, disclose or distribute customer information.
- Not contact customers for personal or unrelated business.
- Not retain customer information after it is no longer required for the assignment or a lawful recordkeeping purpose.
- Protect login credentials and devices used to access the platform.
- Immediately report suspected data breaches, unauthorised access or lost devices.
These obligations are subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable data protection laws.

The confidentiality and data protection obligations continue after the Driver's engagement ends.

### 10. COMPANY CONFIDENTIALITY AND BUSINESS INFORMATION
In addition to customer information, you must protect all confidential business information belonging to DropLag.

This includes:

- Merchant and business partner information.
- Internal delivery procedures.
- Pricing, commissions and financial records.
- Dispatch operations and delivery routes.
- Platform credentials and technical information.
- Internal reports and operational communications.
- Business strategies, plans and commercial arrangements.
- Company databases, records and non-public operational information.
You must not disclose, reproduce, sell, distribute, transfer or use such information for personal gain or unauthorised commercial activities.

You must not share confidential Company information with competitors, third parties or individuals who are not authorised to receive it.

You must not use information obtained through your engagement with DropLag to establish, support or promote competing business activities without the Company's written authorisation, to the extent permitted by applicable law.

Company property, including uniforms, identity cards, delivery bags, equipment, documents and devices, must be returned upon request or termination of engagement.

The Driver must not use DropLag's name, logo or branding to represent themselves as an authorised Company representative after their authorisation has ended.

**Legal Consequences of Breach**

Any unauthorised disclosure, copying, distribution, misuse or exploitation of DropLag's confidential business information may constitute a material breach of this Agreement.

Depending on the nature and severity of the breach, the Company reserves the right to:

1. Suspend or terminate the Driver's access to the Platform.
2. Require the immediate return of confidential documents, equipment and Company property.
3. Require the cessation of any unauthorised use or disclosure.
4. Seek appropriate compensation for proven losses directly resulting from the breach, subject to applicable law.
5. Pursue civil or other appropriate legal remedies where justified.
6. Report suspected criminal conduct to the relevant authorities where appropriate.
These obligations continue during and after the Driver's engagement with DropLag ends.

### 11. VEHICLE AND EQUIPMENT REQUIREMENTS
The Driver is responsible for ensuring that their assigned vehicle is safe, maintained and legally authorised for use.

The Company may inspect or request evidence of:

- Vehicle condition.
- Insurance.
- Registration.
- Roadworthiness.
- Required safety equipment.
Any Company-issued equipment must be used only for authorised business activities and must be returned in reasonable condition, subject to fair wear and tear.

### 12. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR OR EMPLOYMENT STATUS
Unless a separate written employment agreement states otherwise, this Agreement is intended to govern a delivery service engagement and does not, by itself, establish an employment relationship.

The actual legal status of the Driver will depend on the true nature of the working arrangement and applicable Nigerian law, not merely the description used in this Agreement.

Nothing in this clause removes any statutory rights or obligations that apply to the Driver or Company.

### 13. MONITORING AND PLATFORM ACCESS
The Driver acknowledges that the Company may process relevant operational information, including:

- Delivery assignment records.
- Pickup and delivery timestamps.
- GPS location information during active assignments, where enabled.
- Proof-of-delivery records.
- Payment and transaction records.
- Complaints and incident reports.
Such information may be used for delivery coordination, security, fraud prevention, customer service, payment reconciliation and lawful compliance purposes.

The Company will handle personal information in accordance with applicable data protection requirements.

### 14. COMPLAINTS AND DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES
Where a complaint or suspected violation is reported, the Company may conduct an investigation.

Depending on the circumstances, the Company may:

- Request an explanation from the Driver.
- Review delivery records and available evidence.
- Temporarily restrict access while investigating a serious safety or security concern.
- Issue a warning.
- Require additional training.
- Suspend or terminate the Driver's platform access.
The Driver will ordinarily be given an opportunity to respond before a final disciplinary decision is made, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect persons, property, customers or the integrity of the platform.

### 15. SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION
The Company may suspend or terminate a Driver's access where the Driver:

1. Violates this Agreement.
2. Provides false registration information.
3. Engages in theft, fraud or dishonesty.
4. Repeatedly fails to complete accepted assignments without reasonable explanation.
5. Endangers customers, members of the public or other road users.
6. Misuses Company or customer information.
7. Fails to maintain legally required licences or insurance.
8. Engages in conduct that materially damages the Company's reputation or operations.
A Driver may voluntarily discontinue their engagement by providing written notice in accordance with any applicable engagement arrangement.

Upon termination, the Driver must return Company property, complete outstanding remittances, account for parcels in their custody and cease using Company systems and branding.

Termination does not remove any existing lawful payment, confidentiality or liability obligations.

### 16. INDEMNITY AND LIABILITY
To the extent permitted by applicable law, the Driver shall be responsible for losses, damages, claims or expenses directly caused by the Driver's proven negligence, wilful misconduct, fraud, theft or material breach of this Agreement.

The Driver shall not be responsible for losses caused by the Company's negligence, circumstances beyond the Driver's reasonable control or other causes for which the Driver is not legally liable.

Nothing in this Agreement excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded under Nigerian law.

### 17. FORCE MAJEURE
Neither party shall be treated as having breached this Agreement where performance is prevented by circumstances reasonably beyond its control, including severe weather, flooding, civil disturbances, government restrictions, major road closures or other exceptional events.

The affected party must notify the other party as soon as reasonably practicable and take reasonable steps to reduce the impact.

### 18. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
The parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith discussions and written communication.

Where a dispute cannot be resolved amicably, either party may seek appropriate legal remedies before a court of competent jurisdiction in Nigeria.

Nothing in this clause prevents either party from approaching a relevant regulatory authority or seeking urgent legal relief where necessary.

### 19. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and applicable laws of Lagos State where relevant.

### 20. AMENDMENTS
The Company may update these terms where reasonably necessary to reflect changes in its operations, platform functionality or applicable law.

Material changes will be communicated to Drivers through an appropriate notice.

Continued use of the platform after the effective date of an updated Agreement will constitute acceptance only where the Driver has been properly notified and has been given an opportunity to review and accept the updated terms, subject to applicable law.

### 21. SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue to apply to the extent permitted by law.

### 22. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Agreement, together with any applicable written driver engagement terms, payment schedules, privacy notices and operational policies, constitutes the agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter covered herein.

Where a separate signed agreement conflicts with this document, the parties shall interpret the documents together and give effect to the specific written provision applicable to the subject matter, subject to mandatory law.
